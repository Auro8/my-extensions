## ➕ How to Add This Repo

> **Step 1** — Open **Mihon** on your Android device
>
> **Step 2** — Go to **More → Settings → Browse → Extension repos**
>
> **Step 3** — Tap the **+** button and paste this URL:https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Auro8/my-extensions/repo/index.min.json

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Why does Z-Library show a WebView?**
> Z-Library uses Cloudflare protection. Just complete the check once in WebView and it works!

**Q: Can I request a new source?**
> Yes! Open an issue on the [source repo](https://github.com/Auro8/my-extensions-source/issues)

**Q: Is this safe to use?**
> Yes, all extensions are open source and can be reviewed freely.
